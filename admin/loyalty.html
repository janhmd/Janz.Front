Welcome discount

Applied once, on a new customer's first order ("Sign in, and it gets cheaper" on the site).

How it works

  • The level depends on what the customer has spent on delivered orders.
  • The level's discount is applied to every order automatically.
  • A coupon and the level discount do not add up: the bigger one is used.
  • The level name is shown next to the customer's reviews.
