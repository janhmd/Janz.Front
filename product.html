Storage

At room temperature

In a closed pack at room temperature, coffee keeps for 1–2 months.

After you open the pack that time gets shorter, so buy only as much as you will drink in one or two weeks.

After grinding, freshness fades in minutes, not days. Grind the beans right before brewing.

In the freezer

In a closed pack in the freezer, coffee keeps for a long time.

Keep it away from other food, or the coffee can take on that smell. You can freeze it, but thaw it only once, for 10–12 hours at room temperature before brewing. Do not freeze it again.
