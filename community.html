We are extending summer with a new strawberry infusion.

Infusion is a fermentation method where juice of other berries or fruit is added to coffee cherries. This boosts the sweetness and fruitiness of the coffee.

While processing this lot from Sumatra, strawberry was added to the coffee cherries. That is how Indonesia Sumatra Strawberry Karo came out: a bright coffee with notes of strawberry, cherry, cream caramel and milk chocolate.
